## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### General Usage Questions

#### **Q: Is Toolspy's image compressor completely free to use?**
**A:** Yes, our image compressor is completely free with no hidden costs, registration requirements, or usage limitations. You can compress unlimited images without any restrictions.

#### **Q: How much can I reduce my image file size?**
**A:** Typically, you can achieve 50-80% file size reduction while maintaining excellent visual quality. The actual compression ratio depends on your image type, content, and quality settings. Our advanced algorithms ensure optimal compression for each image.

#### **Q: Is it safe to upload my images to Toolspy?**
**A:** Absolutely! Our image compressor uses client-side processing, meaning your images are compressed directly in your browser and never uploaded to our servers. This ensures complete privacy and security for your sensitive images.

#### **Q: What image formats does the compressor support?**
**A:** We support all major image formats including JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, BMP, and TIFF. You can also convert between formats during compression for maximum flexibility.

### Technical Questions

#### **Q: How does batch compression work?**
**A:** You can select up to 10 images simultaneously. Our tool processes each image individually using the same settings, providing consistent results across your entire batch. Progress is tracked in real-time.

#### **Q: What's the difference between lossy and lossless compression?**
**A:** Lossy compression (like JPEG) removes some image data to achieve smaller file sizes, while lossless compression (like PNG) preserves all original data. Our tool automatically applies the best method for your chosen format.

#### **Q: Can I resize images while compressing them?**
**A:** Yes! Our advanced settings allow you to resize images to specific dimensions while compressing. You can maintain aspect ratio or set custom width and height values.

#### **Q: What is progressive JPEG encoding?**
**A:** Progressive JPEG allows images to load in multiple passes, showing a low-quality version first that gradually improves. This enhances user experience on websites by providing immediate visual feedback.

### Quality & Performance Questions

#### **Q: How do I choose the right quality setting?**
**A:** For web images, 75-85% quality usually provides the best balance. For photos intended for printing, use 90-95%. For thumbnails or previews, 60-75% is often sufficient. Use our presets as starting points.

#### **Q: Why choose Toolspy over other free image compressors?**
**A:** Toolspy offers professional-grade features like target file size control, selective EXIF removal, progressive encoding, and client-side processing. Many competitors lack these advanced features or require paid upgrades.

#### **Q: Can I compress images for specific platforms like Instagram or Facebook?**
**A:** Yes! Use our "Web Optimized" preset or customize settings for social media requirements. We recommend 1080x1080px for Instagram posts and under 100KB for optimal loading.

#### **Q: Does compression affect image metadata?**
**A:** You have full control over metadata handling. Choose to remove all EXIF data for smaller files, preserve everything, or use selective removal to keep important data while removing sensitive information.

### Troubleshooting Questions

#### **Q: Why is my compressed image larger than the original?**
**A:** This can happen with PNG images that are already highly optimized or when converting from JPEG to PNG. Try using a lower quality setting or maintaining the original format.

#### **Q: What should I do if compression fails?**
**A:** Ensure your image file isn't corrupted and is under 50MB. Try refreshing the page or using a different browser. Contact our support if issues persist.

#### **Q: Can I undo compression or recover original quality?**
**A:** Compression is permanent. Always keep backups of your original images. We recommend testing settings on a copy before processing important files.
